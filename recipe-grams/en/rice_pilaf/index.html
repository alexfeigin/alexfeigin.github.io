Rice Pilaf

Ingredients:

  • 300g jasmine rice, or another long grain rice
  • 1 large onion, small dice
  • 1 large carrot, grated on the small holes
  • Reserved chicken fat from Grilled Chicken Thighs
  • Reserved chicken cooking juices from Grilled Chicken Thighs
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Cumin
  • Water
  • Optional: cooked chicken pieces from Grilled Chicken Thighs

Instructions

  • Make Grilled Chicken Thighs, and save the pan liquids.
  • Chill the pan liquids in the fridge until they separate.
  • The fat / schmaltz / olive oil will solidify on top, and the brown cooking juices will set underneath.
  • Use the fat layer to cook the onion and carrot.
  • Use the brown cooking juices later as part of the rice cooking liquid.
  • Rinse the rice:
    • Rinse once.
    • Wash in a colander under running water.
    • Rinse a second time.
    • Wash again in a colander under running water.
  • Add the reserved chicken fat, onion, carrot, and a tiny amount of water to the rice cooker.
  • Salt the onion and carrot.
  • Run the rice cooker on a 30 minute program, until the onion and carrot become translucent and start to lightly fry / turn golden.
  • Add the rinsed rice to the rice cooker.
  • Add the reserved chicken cooking juices.
  • Add black pepper and cumin.
  • Add water to slightly above the normal 300g / 2 cup rice line, because the onion and carrot take up some of the volume in the pot.
  • Salt again.
  • Cook using the rice cooker program that matches the rice.
  • When the rice cooker finishes, fluff the rice with rice paddles.
  • If using chicken pieces, mix them into the rice after cooking.
