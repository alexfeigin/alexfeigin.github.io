Chicken Mince Mixture

A chicken mince mixture for oven-baked chicken meatballs or as a filling for pelmeni dough.

Ingredients

  • 1000g boneless chicken thigh mince (or 500g thigh mince and 500g breast mince for a leaner option)
  • 750g finely chopped onion (weight before cooking; 75% of the meat weight)
  • About 100g ice cubes
  • A little olive oil, if sweating the onions in a pan
  • Water, as needed while sweating the onions in a pan
  • 13g salt
  • Black pepper, granulated garlic, and cayenne pepper, to taste (also works with grill rub)

I usually use only boneless thigh mince for a richer, softer texture. I use half thigh mince and half breast mince only when I want a leaner option, as breast mince has very little fat.

Instructions

  1. Precook the onions using either method:
    • Pan: Sweat the onions with a little olive oil until all the onions have softened. Add a little water periodically as needed to keep them from burning or browning before the rest have softened.
    • Microwave: I microwave the chopped onions in a microwave-safe dish for 4–5 minutes, until softened.
  2. Transfer the hot onions from the pan or microwave to a mixing bowl and immediately add about 100g ice cubes. Stir and let the ice melt. Keep the resulting liquid: the ice helps cool the onions and adds water to the mince mixture, which works especially well for pelmeni filling. Let the onions cool before adding the meat.
  3. Mix the cooled onions and their liquid with the chicken mince, salt, and spices using your hands (gloves are recommended).

Uses

  • Follow the oven-baked chicken meatballs recipe to shape and bake the mixture. One batch of this mixture fills about 1 oven tray; the meatballs recipe uses a double batch for 2 trays.
  • Use as a filling with the pelmeni dough recipe. This is a large batch of filling; use only as much as needed for the amount of dough you prepare.
  • If you make the mixture for pelmeni and have some filling left over, shape it into meatballs and cook them in the oven or air fryer.
