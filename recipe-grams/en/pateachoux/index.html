Pâte à choux

Ingredients

  • 60 g milk
  • 16 g butter
  • small pinch salt
  • 30 g ap flour
  • 1 large egg

Ingredients X 2

  • 120 g milk
  • 32 g butter
  • medium pinch salt
  • 60 g ap flour
  • 2 large eggs

Ingredients X 3

  • 180 grams milk
  • 50 g butter
  • pinch of salt
  • 90 grams ap flour
  • 3 large eggs

last egg might be too large, we can beat the egg and add it little by little to get wanted consistency of dough
